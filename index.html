Hi, I’m Sharry.

I started lifting to get through a hard year, and found out that getting stronger changes far more than your body. Now I help other people find that feeling.

My clients are busy. Some are rebuilding after an injury or a pregnancy, some want to lift their first real weight, some just want to stop feeling stiff. They all want a plan that makes sense, and someone in their corner who notices the small wins.

How it works

  1. Get in touch

    Send me a message about your goals, your history and your week. No pressure to sign up.

  2. Assessment

    We test how you move, where you’re strong and what needs care. This is your starting line.

  3. Your plan

    A programme written for you, with sessions that fit the time you really have.

  4. Train and review

    We train, check in every week and re-test every six. You’ll see your progress in the numbers.

Questions people ask

I’ve never trained before. Is that okay?

Yes, and it’s one of my favourite places to start. We’ll learn each movement properly before adding any weight.

Where do sessions happen?

In-person sessions take place at a studio that I arrange. Online clients train wherever suits them, at a gym or at home, with minimal equipment.

Do I need to change my diet?

Not unless you want to. If you do, the nutrition plan focuses on simple habits, not meal plans or counting everything.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You can cancel at the end of your current booked period, as long as you let me know in advance.
