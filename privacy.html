Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you visit this website or contact me, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

In short

  • This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • Your personal data is only processed when you contact me, and only to reply to you.
  • Your data is never sold or used for marketing.

1. Who is responsible

The controller responsible for data processing on this website is:

Fitnex
Email: sharareh.fit@gmail.com

2. Visiting this website (hosting)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical information to the server, which may be stored in server log files:

  • your IP address
  • date and time of the request
  • the page requested and the referring page
  • browser type and operating system

This is necessary to deliver the website and to keep it secure and stable. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in providing a working, secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not combine this data with other data and cannot identify you from it.

GitHub may process this data in the USA. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

3. Fonts

This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. To display the fonts, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which transmits your IP address to Google. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in a consistent and readable design (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA, which is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Contact form and email

If you use the contact form or email me, I process the details you provide: your name, your email address, the service you’re interested in and your message.

I use this data only to answer your enquiry and, if you wish, to arrange training. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For general questions that are not about training with me, the legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering messages sent to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Messages sent through the form are delivered to my email inbox by the form service Web3Forms, which processes the data on my behalf. More information: Web3Forms Privacy Policy.

Providing your data is voluntary, but without your name and email address I can’t reply to you.

5. Health information

Training often involves health topics such as injuries, pregnancy or medical conditions. This is a special category of personal data (Art. 9 GDPR). Please don’t send detailed health information through the contact form or by email. We can talk about it in person when we first meet.

If you choose to share health information with me, I process it only to plan safe training for you, on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time with effect for the future.

6. How long data is kept

Enquiries are deleted once they have been fully dealt with, and no later than six months after our last contact, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as needed for our training relationship and for any statutory retention periods, for example under tax law.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device. That’s why there is no cookie banner.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data I hold about you (Art. 15)
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16)
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17)
  • restrict how your data is processed (Art. 18)
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21)
  • withdraw any consent you have given, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3))

To use any of these rights, email sharareh.fit@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place (Art. 77 GDPR).

9. Automated decisions

I don’t use automated decision-making or profiling.

10. Security

This website is only available over an encrypted HTTPS connection, so data you send through the form is encrypted in transit.

11. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page.
